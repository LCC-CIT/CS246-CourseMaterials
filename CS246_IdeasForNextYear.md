# CS246 Ideas for Next Year



## Teaching and Lecture Notes

### 2. Agile and User Stories

Get some clarity about how to enter user stories in Jira. Should the title be the full user story, or should that be in the description?

Emphasize that user stories describe what a user would do. They are not meant to describe what a developer needs to implement--that is what the sub-tasks are for.

Explain that the ...so that clause is there to help you to think about why the user is doing this. Sometimes it might seem obvious and silly to include, but include it anyway so you know you thought about the purpose.

### 5. Working with a Client

Give instruction about how to be courteous and professional.

- How to schedule client meetings.
- How to communicate via email or other means (telephone!?) like Zoom and the issue of having cameras on.
- Having an attitude that you are serving them and that you want to make the site they want.

Tell the story about the experience at TriQuint working with Northern Telcom. Add caveats about personality and generational differences, but emphasize that personal relationship relationship and trust matter in business and technical work.

### 6. UX Diagrams

Give a demo of how to show UX diagrams to a client in order to get good feedback&mdash;having the client walk through the web page wireframes, following the user stories, to validate that the user stories and wireframes represent what they want.

Check out the possibility of making Figma prototypes that a client could navigate.
